# Trained models

The trained weights are **not included** in this repository.

The YOLOv5s, YOLOv8s and SQRT-balanced YOLOv8s checkpoints (`best.pt`, about 14 MB for YOLOv5s and 22 MB for YOLOv8s) were saved to Google Drive during training in Colab. They may be added later as a GitHub Release asset. Until then, the notebooks in `code/notebooks/` show exactly how they were trained.
